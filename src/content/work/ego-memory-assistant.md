A prototype of an assistant for people with mild cognitive impairment or early Alzheimer's disease. The glasses' camera turns what you see into a short text diary, one entry every 15 seconds, and a language model answers questions from that diary, pointing to the moment its answer comes from.

It runs on recordings and live, from Pupil Labs Neon glasses. The build log takes it apart on 500 benchmark questions: how much the diary adds over a reader with no memory, what kinds of false lines the describer writes, and what it costs to store and to write the diary live.
