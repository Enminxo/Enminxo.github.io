abstract: "Egocentric Natural Language Query (NLQ) grounding asks a model to localize, in a long first-person video, the temporal interval that answers a free-form text query. Existing methods fuse video appearance with the query but ignore hand motion, even though roughly 41% of Ego4D NLQ queries are answered at a moment of hand-object manipulation or its immediate outcome. We propose a hand-trajectory encoder that converts a sequence of hand skeletons into semantic hand kinematic features, which are aligned and combined with pretrained video-text features through cross-attention fusion with adaptive gating. On the Ego4D NLQ v2 validation split, the clearest gains appear for Hand-Object Interaction queries (+2.54 R1@IoU=0.3) and Quantity/State queries (+4.32 R1@IoU=0.3)."
