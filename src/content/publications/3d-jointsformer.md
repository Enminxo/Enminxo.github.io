title: "Real-time monocular skeleton-based hand gesture recognition using 3D-Jointsformer"
authors: "Enmin Zhong, Carlos R. Del-Blanco, Daniel Berjón, Fernando Jaureguizar, Narciso García"
venue: "MDPI Sensors"
year: 2023
abstract: "Automatic hand gesture recognition in video sequences has widespread applications, ranging from home automation to sign language interpretation and clinical operations. A hybrid approach combining 3D Convolutional Neural Networks (3D-CNNs) and Transformers is proposed: a 3D-CNN computes high-level semantic skeleton embeddings capturing local spatial and temporal characteristics, while a Transformer with self-attention efficiently captures long-range temporal dependencies. Evaluation on the Briareo and Multimodal Hand Gesture datasets achieved accuracy scores of 95.49% and 97.25% respectively, with real-time performance on a standard CPU."
